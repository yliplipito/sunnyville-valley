# 🌞 Sunnyville Valley 🌻

Welcome to **Sunnyville Valley**, the happiest, coziest 3D village where the sun is always shining! 🌸✨🎈

Explore the charming town square, talk to friendly villagers, pick golden sunflowers, bake delicious tarts, and pet cute animals in this delightful relaxing adventure!

---

## 🎈 Meet Your Neighbors

- 🎩 **Mayor Barnaby**: The cheerful mayor who loves welcoming new friends to town!
- 🌸 **Daisy the Florist**: A kind-hearted gardener with a passion for golden sunflowers.
- 🧁 **Baker Benny**: Master baker at Sunshine Bakery famous for fresh warm berry tarts!
- 👴 **Old Man Gregory**: The wise village elder resting peacefully on the plaza bench.
- 🎈 **Little Timmy**: An energetic boy who loves playing games and red balloons.
- 🐶 **Buster the Dog**: The friendliest, fluffiest pup in the valley! (Don't forget to give him pets and find his lost squeaky toy!)

---

## 🎮 How to Play

- **Move**: `W`, `A`, `S`, `D` or `Arrow Keys`
- **Look Around**: `Mouse` (First-Person Pointer Lock)
- **Interact / Talk**: `E` or `Left-Click`
- **Jump**: `Space`
- **Sprint**: Hold `Shift`
- **Sound Toggle**: Click 🔊 / 🔇 in the top-right corner
- **Fullscreen**: Click ⛶ for the full immersive experience

---

## 🌟 Features

- **Cozy 3D Town**: Beautiful pastel houses, cobblestone paths, dancing flowerbeds, and a peaceful town fountain.
- **Village Activities**: Ring the festival morning bell, make wishes at the fountain, read the town bulletin board, and find lost dog toys.
- **Heartwarming Quests**: Help your neighbors, deliver gifts, and explore the sunny valley at your own pace.
- **Original Cheerful Soundtrack**: Relaxing marimba and acoustic melodies inspired by classic wholesome games.
- **Expressive Village Dialogue**: Full Animalese speech sound effects for every friendly villager!
